Month by month: investments stacked in the same column

Same chart as today (earned above the line, spent below, dot = kept). The ↗ Investments chip in the header works like the one on Categories: off by default. When on, each month's column gets violet segments stacked on the outside: what you cashed out on top of Earned, what you bought under Spent. The dot stays Kept = Earned − Spent either way (investing counts as saving), so turning the chip on never moves the line. With the chip on, a dashed blue line adds Liquidity left = Earned + cashed out − Spent − bought. Click the chip to toggle.
