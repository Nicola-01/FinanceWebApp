Login, three proposals

Same fields and actions as the current login, three compositions. Each shows a 1280×800 desktop frame and a 375×760 phone frame. The demo button appears only when demo mode is on, as an optional secondary action.

  • Registration is invite-only, so there is no sign-up link, only a quiet invite line.
  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Instrument Sans for text inputs, JetBrains Mono with tabular numerals for amounts.
  • Flat by design: no glows, halos, blurred blobs, animated backgrounds or glass. The brand gradient is on the primary button only.
A

Split screen

Recommended

Why recommended: the form keeps the calm reading order of the current login, and the right half gives the screen a real product presence without decoration. A sample wallet card shows the app's core object instead of a generic illustration. On a phone the panel shrinks to one card above the form.

B

Centred, with presence

Trade-off: the most branded of the three. A fanned stack of wallet colours and a large display heading give the page a personality the current card lacks, while the form stays in one calm column. The stack costs height above the form on phones, so it is scaled down rather than dropped.

C

Editorial

Trade-off: the headline and a faded ledger of sample transactions carry the story, and the form sits quietly on the right. It is the most distinctive of the three and the one that says what the app does. The ledger is decoration only, so on a phone it is cut to three rows.
