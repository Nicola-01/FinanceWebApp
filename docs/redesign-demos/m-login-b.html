Login, B background variants

Proposal B (centred, with presence) with four page backgrounds. Only the atmosphere changes, plus small tweaks to the wallet stack where it helps it sit in the scene. Each variant shows a 1280×800 desktop frame and a 375×760 phone frame.

  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Instrument Sans for text inputs, JetBrains Mono with tabular numerals for amounts.
  • Flat only: no glows, halos, blurred blobs, glass, or animated backgrounds. The grain in B1 is a static SVG noise layer.
  • Every variant is defined for both light and dark. The toggle switches all mocks on the page.
B1

Colour field

Recommended

Why it works: the brand gradient gives the top half of the page a real place to stand, so the heading and wallet fan read as part of a scene instead of floating on grey. The fan and the form card straddle the band edge, and the grain stays a faint static noise rather than texture for its own sake.

B2

Ledger pattern

Why it works: the page becomes a faint ledger of amounts and categories, which ties the login to what the app actually shows. The texture fades out under the form, so the centre stays clean and the heading still reads as the main element.

B3

Scattered wallets

Why it works: flat wallet cards at full colour, cropped by the frame edges, make the page feel populated by the user's own objects. The centre fan is untouched, and on a phone three cards peek in from the corners.

B4

Grid and colour corner

Why it works: a fine dot grid gives the page a structured, drafting-table quality, and two solid arcs in wallet colours anchor opposite corners. It is the most restrained option of the four and the easiest to live with every day.
