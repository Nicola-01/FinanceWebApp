Flagging a tag as an investment: three proposals

The Manage Categories drawer (phone full-screen, desktop side drawer). "Investments" is flagged; its children PAC and ETF inherit; "Pension fund" under "Savings" is flagged on its own. Inherited children can't be toggled (no opt-out), they show where the flag comes from.

Pickers (same in every proposal)

In the transaction tag picker a flagged tag, and every tag under it, gets a small violet ↗ after the name. No extra text, so the picker stays compact.
