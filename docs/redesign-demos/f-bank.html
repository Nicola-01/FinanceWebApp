Bank accounts and rules: where they live, and how the list scales

Same data as today (2 accounts, 6 rules). Each rule row is a single line: name, then the bank text it matches, then the category. Rename, regex and "used by a subscription" become small marks at the end of the row. Edit and delete move into the editor that opens on tap. Account actions move into a ⋯ menu, so Unlink is no longer the most visible button.

A. A small card in Settings that opens a Bank screen recommended

Settings stays short. The card opens the shared ResponsiveOverlay shell: a side drawer on desktop, full screen on a phone. No new kind of window.

With many rules: search, or group by category

Grouping only turns on past a threshold, for example 15 rules. With 6 rules it isn't worth it. Searching narrows the list as you type and matches the name, the bank text and the category.

B. Bank as its own section of the wallet

Same content as the Bank screen in A, but it opens from the More menu on a phone (and as a seventh top tab on desktop). It only shows when Open Banking is on. It is easier to find, but the tab bar gets more crowded.

C. Add on top of A or B: "Create rule" from a transaction to categorise

You pick a category for an imported transaction, then one tap saves it as a rule. The editor opens pre-filled with the bank text and the category you just chose, and shows how many other pending transactions it would match.
