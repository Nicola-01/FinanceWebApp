Landing, benefits first: two variants

Same narrative, two looks. Each variant has a 1080px desktop frame and a 375px phone frame, rendered from one shared markup block. The story goes from the promise, to the problem the owner liked, to the outcome each feature delivers, then trust and a final call to action.

  • Copy is new, written for outcomes. Feature names stay visible as the small proof under each benefit. Figures in the proof fragments are examples, and the hero and month numbers are labelled as examples.
  • Problem band kept from ProblemBand.tsx and restyled to v2 (icon tiles, mono sums). Brand gradient only on the primary CTA; the wallet purple is a solid fill.
  • Bank sync is marked "Preview" because the live landing still says "Coming soon". Update that pill once the feature is announced.
  • No glows, halos, blobs, or animated wordmark. Icons are inline SVG. Fonts are self-hosted from fonts/. No CDN.
1

Dark, high contrast

Recommended

Why recommended: dark is the app's default, and a solid purple wallet card, sum box and final band carry the bold tone the brief asks for without a single glow. The benefit blocks read as bold cards, so each outcome stands on its own.

2

Light, editorial

Trade-off: a large display headline, numbered benefit rows on hairlines, and white proof cards on the warm paper background. It reads as a magazine feature rather than a product page, so it sells the story more than the screens. Slightly less punchy than V1 on a phone.
